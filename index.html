Hermes Personal connects my self-hosted Hermes assistant to my Google account, so I can review email and manage my calendar through conversation.

This integration is for my own use. This website provides its app information and privacy disclosures.

01

Read my email

Find messages, read email, and summarize what needs attention. Gmail access is read-only: this integration cannot send, modify, or delete email.

02

Manage my calendar

Review upcoming events and availability, and create, update, or remove calendar events when I ask.

How it works

I authorize access directly with Google. Hermes uses that permission on my private server, and relevant content may be sent to OpenAI to answer my requests. This static website does not sign anyone in or receive Google credentials or account content.

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. The privacy policy explains data processing, storage, and deletion.
